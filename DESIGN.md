## Style Prompt

A graceful school-sports video for rhythmic gymnastics: clean French classroom energy, fluid movement, warm stage light, and precise rhythmic counting. The visual language should feel personal and encouraging, with ribbon-like motion, apparatus photography, and enough polish for a short family or class presentation.

## Colors

- Deep plum background: `#24142f`
- Warm spotlight: `#f6d28a`
- Ribbon coral: `#ff5a76`
- Practice mint: `#93e2c7`
- Chalk white text: `#fff8ea`

## Typography

- Display: `Fraunces`, heavy, for graceful title moments.
- Supporting text: `IBM Plex Sans`, light to medium, for school-presentation clarity.

## Motion

- Use ribbon sweeps, soft zooms, blur crossfades, and count-based pulsing.
- Keep movement fluid and controlled; avoid harsh cuts.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use default blue corporate colors.
- Do not make the video look like a generic sports promo.
- Do not crowd the screen with long paragraphs.
- Do not use jump cuts between scenes.
